FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

"Every patient is treated as an individual — your comfort, privacy, and results are our highest priority from the very first consultation."

Is Grace & Light Cosmetic Clinic DHA licensed?

add

Yes, Grace & Light Cosmetic Clinic LLC is fully licensed by the Dubai Health Authority (DHA). All our physicians hold valid DHA licenses and the clinic operates in full compliance with UAE health regulations and international safety standards.

What insurance providers do you accept?

add

We accept Daman, Thiqa, AXA, and Cigna insurance for covered medical treatments. Please contact us in advance to confirm coverage for your specific procedure, as coverage varies by plan and treatment type.

Is there private parking available at the clinic?

add

Yes, our villa setting at Al Wasl Road includes dedicated private parking for patients. We value your discretion and privacy, and our location has been designed to ensure a comfortable, exclusive arrival experience.

How do I book a consultation?

add

You can book a consultation by calling us at +971 4 338 5558, sending us a WhatsApp message, filling out our contact form on this page, or visiting us directly at Villa #3, Al Wasl Road, Umm Al Sheif. We are open Saturday–Thursday, 10 AM–7 PM (Saturday from 9 AM). Friday is our rest day.

What can I expect from a Profhilo treatment?

add

Profhilo is a bio-remodelling injectable that deeply hydrates the skin and stimulates collagen and elastin production. The treatment consists of two sessions spaced 4 weeks apart. Results include significantly improved skin texture, firmness, and luminosity with minimal downtime.

What are your operating hours?

add

We are open Saturday 9 AM – 7 PM, Sunday through Thursday 10 AM – 7 PM. We are closed on Fridays. For urgent inquiries outside business hours, you may send us a WhatsApp message and our team will respond as soon as possible.

Profhilo — Our Signature Bioremodeller

Profhilo is one of the highest concentrations of hyaluronic acid available in an injectable product. Unlike traditional fillers, it spreads under the skin to remodel and hydrate from within, stimulating the production of collagen and elastin. Results become visible after 4 weeks, peak at 8 weeks, and last 6–12 months.

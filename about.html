Who We Are

A sanctuary of grace and clinical excellence

Grace & Light Cosmetic Clinic LLC is Dubai's premier multi-specialty aesthetic and plastic surgery medical centre, nestled in a private villa on Al Wasl Road, Umm Al Sheif. We were founded with a singular vision: to deliver world-class aesthetic medicine with the warmth, discretion, and personal attention that every patient deserves.

Our private villa setting is not merely aesthetic — it is a deliberate choice. In a city of grand hospitals and busy clinics, Grace & Light offers something rare: a truly intimate, exclusive experience where your comfort, privacy, and results are the only priorities.
